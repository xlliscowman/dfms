01 — Miusenphytech

Treating plants as a methodology.

Miusenphytech is a national high-tech enterprise focused on the research and creation of plant-based cosmetic actives. Guided by TCM theory and grounded in skin wellness and surface physiology, we combine modern science with consumer needs and product realities to develop “thoughtful plant actives”, supplying cosmetic companies with quality ingredients and custom development.

We do three things: find it, prove it, make it usable. From the Pinggu line to 11 plant bases, none of this chain is outsourced.
