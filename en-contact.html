04 — FAQ

You may also want to ask.

Do samples cost anything?

Standard samples are free, freight collect; large and custom samples are charged at cost, with the amount confirmed before shipping. A company can request more than once a year.

Is there an MOQ?

Standard ingredients start at 1 kg, some custom items at 5 kg. Tiered pricing comes with the quote — the more you use, the lower the unit price.

Can you customise?

Yes. Plant source, molecular range and blend can all be adjusted. From sample to production usually takes 6–10 weeks, depending on the ingredient and process.

Will my formula stay confidential?

Yes. We sign an NDA as standard; formula details stay with the technical and sales contacts and never enter public material.
