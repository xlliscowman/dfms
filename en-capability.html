03 — Efficacy / prove it

From cell to human, four layers of evidence.

Each layer answers one question. Formulators don’t want adjectives; they want methods, data and sample sizes they can check.

Layer 01

Biochemistry & cells

Enzyme activity and cell models — first, does the activity exist.

Layer 02

Tissue & 3D skin

Penetration, barrier and structural change, observed on skin models close to human.

Layer 03

Human efficacy

Instrumental measurement plus self-assessment, with duration and sample size stated.

Layer 04

Clinical

Aesthetic-clinic settings and physician assessment, testing performance under extreme conditions.
